### Hi, I'm Andrey 👋

Software developer with 15+ years of commercial work, mostly Ruby on Rails. For most of that time I was a long-term contractor for small businesses: I built and ran a classifieds service for heavy machinery and a system that automates critical business processes for a small group of companies. I owned the whole cycle — from shaping the task with the client to launch, support, and growth.

The last year, working with coding agents, I build my own products and small open-source tools around [RubyLLM](https://github.com/crmne/ruby_llm).

Based in Novi Sad, Serbia. **Open to work.**

### What I'm building

- **[DropKind](https://dropkind.app)** — send web articles to Kindle from your browser or phone, without the clutter. Live in the App Store.
- **[Duck](https://duck.study)** — learn the words of tonight's episode before you press play, then keep them with spaced repetition. Preparing for release.
